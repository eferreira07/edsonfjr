AI Consulting · AI Workshops · AI Training

I help teams adopt AI through consulting, workshops, and training

My career started as a consultant — sitting alongside teams, understanding their challenges, and helping turn technology from something complicated into something people could actually use. Over the past 20+ years, I've worked with organizations ranging from growing businesses to Fortune 500 companies, helping teams adopt new technologies and improve how they work. Today, I lead product initiatives focused on AI enablement, helping organizations understand how to use AI effectively and responsibly. I grew up moving between Brazil, Portugal, Chile, Argentina, Peru, Mexico, and the United States. That taught me one thing: technology only creates impact when people can understand it. If something feels too complicated, people won't use it.
