# Quote request → estimate

## Voice-over script for ElevenLabs

Another quote lands in the inbox. The owner needs to move customer details, service, and pricing into QuickBooks, then repeat the same prompt.

With Quick Actions, he clicks Create an Estimate. Clear instructions and this quote are ready for ChatGPT. He checks the message, then sends.

ChatGPT checks connected email and saved pricing, then prepares the customer and estimate. It flags an uncertain price or customer match rather than guessing.

The owner reviews the result and sends the estimate. The team shares the same steps, so no one starts from a blank prompt.
