How it works

One task. Clear steps. Your choice of AI.

Build a dependable starting point for work you repeat. Quick Actions prepares the prompt; you add the live context and decide what happens next.

  1. 1

    Choose or create a task

    Browse the library, search by purpose, or keep a task you’ve made your own.

  2. 2

    Add the details that change

    Fill in the context for today and review the prepared instructions before opening them.

  3. 3

    Open it in your AI app

    Continue in ChatGPT, Codex, or Claude. Review the AI’s response and choose what to use or send.
